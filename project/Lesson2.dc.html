What is WCAG 2.2?

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines are published by the W3C, the international body for web standards. Version 2.2 was published in October 2023. PSBAR 2018 points to WCAG level AA, so it’s the yardstick for your Moodle pages, documents and media.

Conformance levels

Success criteria are graded A, AA or AAA. Levels are cumulative: to meet AA you must meet every A criterion too.

A

Essential

Without these, some people can’t use the content at all.

  • Alt text for images
  • Captions on recorded video
  • Everything works by keyboard
Legal target AA

Standard

Removes the most common, significant barriers. Required by PSBAR 2018.

  • 4.5:1 text contrast
  • Audio description for recorded video
  • Text resizes to 200%
AAA

Enhanced

Goes further. Worth meeting where you can, but not required for whole sites.

  • 7:1 text contrast
  • Sign language for video
  • Plain reading level
